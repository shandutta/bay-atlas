BEHIND THE MAP

A local estimate,
grounded in sold homes.

Colors represent estimated dollars per interior square foot. The gradient is continuous: a warmer shade means a higher estimated price, and transparent areas lack enough nearby evidence.

Which homes count?

Detached single-family homes with at least two bedrooms, sold within the last two years. The model favors homes near your selected size. Every comparable links back to its source record.

Nearby, similar, and supported

Closer sales and homes of a similar size contribute more. The model expands its local search only as needed, up to 3 km, and reduces the influence of unusual prices. At least three nearby comparable sales and an effective sample of two are needed to show an estimate.

Read the range, too

The displayed range reflects variation among comparable sales. It is not a guaranteed sale price or an appraisal. Condition, renovations, lot, views, micro-location, and timing can materially change the result.

Source: dated sold-home records. Data availability is shown on the map.
